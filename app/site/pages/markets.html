How a market works.

  • Its own honeycomb. Each market has its own board of hexes, its own price bands and its own signed multipliers. The rules on How it works are the same for both.
  • A live price index. The price is an index of several exchanges plus Perpl's order book on Monad, sampled every 100 ms and signed column by column for settlement.
  • One balance. Your test USDC credit is shared: a win on one market can be staked on the other straight away. Switch market from the picker at the top of the board.
  • Open bets keep running. Switching market never cancels anything. Bets on the other board still settle, and their results land in your balance and history.
