    A price chart you can tap.

    Hexit lays a honeycomb over a live price. Each column of hexes is a five-second window and each hex one band of price. The price line, the fuse, burns left to right through the board.

    Double-tap a hex and your stake is armed. If the fuse touches that hex at any moment in its window, it goes BOOM and pays your stake times the multiplier printed on it. Hexes far from the price pay more, up to 100x.

    Every tap is a transaction. You pay no gas.

    Hexit runs on Monad testnet, chain {{chainId}}. Your bet is a real transaction you can open on MonadVision, and you never need MON to send it.

    1. Your browser signs

      A test wallet created in this browser signs the bet. No extension, no popup per tap.

    2. Hexit relays it

      Our relayer sends the signed bet to Monad testnet and pays the MON gas.

    3. The contract books it

      HexitGame checks your signature, the signed multiplier and the limits before it takes the stake.

    4. You see the receipt

      A card on the right links the transaction. Settlements, grants, deposits and withdrawals show the same way.
