How to tap trade

Ten seconds to learn.

  1. Pick a stake. Bottom-right pill.The stake bar at the bottom. Starts at $0.10.
  2. Double-tap a hex. Each hex is a price range over about 6.7 seconds. The number on it is your multiplier. Your bet fills half a second later at the live quote, as long as it is within your slippage setting.
  3. Watch the fuse. If the price touches your tile during its window, it goes BOOM and pays stake × multiplier.
  4. Changed your mind? Armed tiles can't be cancelled.
  5. Feed stalls? If the price feed skips more than a quarter of a second while a column is live, every tile in that column the line has not touched is refunded.
